# Theek Karo — Community Guidelines (Phase 18)

Applies to all community surfaces: comments, replies, reactions, follows, civic
initiatives, volunteer opportunities, community groups, evidence contributions,
and public discussions.

## Purpose

Theek Karo is a civic platform for citizens, communities, institutions and
departments to work together toward measurable improvement. Discussions must
remain:

```
NON-PARTISAN
EVIDENCE-BASED
CIVIC-FOCUSED
PRIVACY-RESPECTING
SAFE
INCLUSIVE
TRANSPARENT
```

## Rules

1. **Be constructive.** Contributions should help understand or resolve civic
   issues. Support claims with observations, photos, documents or public sources.
2. **No harassment.** No personal attacks, threats, targeted abuse or coordinated
   harassment of any person or group.
3. **No doxxing.** Never share personal phone numbers, home addresses, private
   contact details or other personal information about anyone.
4. **No spam.** No promotional content, repeated duplicates, or malicious links.
5. **No fabricated evidence.** Community observations are community signals, not
   platform verification. Do not post misleading or fabricated media.
6. **No impersonation.** Do not impersonate officials, institutions, departments
   or other users.
7. **No political campaigning.** The platform does not support partisan
   persuasion, political targeting or campaigning of any kind.
8. **No misinformation campaigns.** Do not use the platform for coordinated
   false-information efforts.

## Trust and verification language

The platform distinguishes these states and expects users to do the same:

```
Reported            — a citizen filed a report
Community Observed  — a user provided an observation
Community Supported — users expressed support (does NOT equal proof)
Platform Verified   — platform verification workflow outcome
Officially Responded— an institution/department responded
Resolution Submitted
Resolution Verified
```

Likes, comments and followers are **not** evidence that an issue is true.

## Moderation pipeline

```
Comment/Contribution
  → Automated safety checks
  → AI recommendation (advisory only)
  → Rule evaluation
  → Human moderator when required
  → Action (warning, removal, restriction, suspension)
  → Appeal
```

AI never bans users autonomously and never declares truth without evidence.
Human moderators handle high-impact decisions.

## Reporting

Users can report comments and users for:

- Harassment
- Spam
- False information
- Personal information exposure
- Abusive content
- Impersonation
- Threats / doxxing
- Off-topic content
- Other

## Appeals

Users may appeal meaningful moderation actions (content removal, restriction,
suspension). Appeals are reviewed by a human moderator; the decision is recorded
and the user is notified.

## Groups

- Groups are civic: geography, civic topics, institution categories or
  initiatives.
- Platform-level safety rules **always override** group rules.
- Groups cannot be used for harassment, doxxing, threats, misinformation,
  political persuasion or coordinated abuse.

## Consequences

Moderation actions are proportionate and recorded in the moderation audit log:
action, reason, policy, moderator, appeal and outcome.
